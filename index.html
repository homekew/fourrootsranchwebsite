Our Story

We are a family of six living in beautiful Bend, Oregon. Our journey with animals began in 2017 when we connected with a rescue organization rehoming alpacas in need.

Moved by their mission, we welcomed 20 alpacas into our care, giving them a loving forever home. These animals quickly captured our hearts, and we realized we had the space and love to help more animals in need.

In 2019, Four Roots Ranch became a nonprofit, allowing us to expand our commitment to animal rescue and care. We have experienced tremendous growth since welcoming our first group of alpacas, but we remain 100% family run.

Today, our sanctuary is home to more than 100 animals, many of them seniors, all of them family.

A forever home for animals in need

Our sanctuary provides a safe haven for animals who have endured abuse, abandonment, and neglect, as well as those whose owners can no longer care for them due to significant life changes.

Specialized camelid care

Alpacas and llamas are our specialty. Many of the animals in our care are seniors or have needs that require patient, individualized attention.

A sanctuary family

Alongside the alpacas and llamas, Four Roots Ranch is home to a small menagerie of sheep, goats, pigs, chickens, ducks, turkeys, and barn cats.

Community support

We share what we have learned through guidance on housing and feeding, medical care questions, adoption referrals, and herd and flock dynamics.

They are here because they are loved.

For many of our animals, Four Roots Ranch is the place where they finally get to grow old safely.
