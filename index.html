Where second chances take root.

Four Roots Ranch provides lifelong care for senior alpacas, llamas, and the animals who have joined our sanctuary family along the way.

Donate Meet the animals

Family-run in Bend, Oregon, Four Roots Ranch is home to more than 100 animals, many of them seniors.

Our work centers on lifelong care, thoughtful education, and helping animals who are too often overlooked.

Built from one rescue into a lifelong promise.

Our journey began in 2017 when we connected with a rescue organization rehoming alpacas in need. We welcomed 20 alpacas into our care, giving them a loving forever home.

Those animals quickly captured our hearts. What started as one rescue grew into a deeper commitment to animals who needed safety, care, and a place to belong.

In 2019, Four Roots Ranch became a nonprofit, allowing us to expand our work while remaining a family-run sanctuary rooted in daily, hands-on care.

Many of our residents are seniors.

Their care is steady, practical, and deeply personal: hay, feed, bedding, veterinary support, shearing, medications, and patient attention day after day.

They are here because they are loved.

What sanctuary care looks like here.

Specialized camelid care

Alpacas and llamas are our specialty. Many need experienced, individualized support as they age.

A wider sanctuary family

Our animals include sheep, goats, pigs, chickens, ducks, turkeys, barn cats, and others who found their way here.

Education and support

We share practical guidance around housing, feeding, medical care questions, adoption referrals, and herd or flock dynamics.

Help keep their care steady.

Every gift helps provide the everyday care our senior and rescued animals depend on: hay, feed, bedding, veterinary appointments, medications, shearing, and safe shelter.
